# v1.0.72 Spatial Structured Footprint Diagnostics

Date: 2026-08-19

## Objective

Add a diagnostic-only metric for broad low-frequency, watermark-geometry-correlated residuals that remain visually visible even when existing structured aligned/global metrics improve.

Real motivating sample after v1.0.71:
- structured mode: `primary+shape-ghost`
- structured before: 1.720
- structured after: 1.546
- structured improvement: 10.1%
- residual density: 4.8%
- temporal donor/atlas inactive
- visible broad diamond-like footprint still present

This demonstrates a metric blind spot rather than a downstream safety failure.

## Diagnostic design

`measureStructuredFootprintResidual(image, alphaMap, options)`:
- low-pass luma radius defaults to 2 pixels to suppress fine texture;
- measures low-frequency image gradients across a broad alpha body/ring band;
- compares image-gradient orientation with watermark/alpha-gradient orientation;
- weights shape-aligned low-frequency energy;
- finds low-alpha samples outside the watermark footprint along the alpha normal;
- discounts candidate energy when a similar gradient continues outside the footprint, reducing false positives from genuine scene/material boundaries;
- returns:
  - `score`: continuity-discounted footprint score;
  - `rawScore`: score before scene-continuity discount;
  - `coverage`;
  - `shapeAlignedDensity`;
  - `continuityMean`;
  - sample counts.

## Runtime integration

Phase 1 is intentionally preview-only:
- `main.js` receives the cleaned preview ROI and the internal detection alpha map;
- the footprint diagnostic is calculated on the final cleaned preview ROI;
- the result is attached under `antiStreak.structured.footprint` before writing the preview JSON;
- no worker, engine, export, donor, structured cleanup, mask, threshold, strength, or pixel behavior changes.

This location was chosen to minimize behavioral risk while allowing immediate validation on the real brown/wood samples.

## UI

The Anti-streak card receives runtime-mounted readonly fields:
- Footprint score
- Footprint raw
- Footprint coverage
- Shape-aligned density
- Continuity discount

The fields are mounted by `calibration-ui.js` when absent, avoiding a hard dependency on a newly deployed static HTML fragment.

## Synthetic regression coverage

1. Broad diamond-correlated tonal ghost must score above a clean background.
2. Clean smooth structured region must remain low.
3. A real scene boundary crossing the watermark must have meaningful continuity and a discounted score below raw score.
4. High-frequency texture without shape-correlated footprint must not dominate the metric.
5. Formatter coverage verifies stable display of all footprint values.

## Safety

- Diagnostic-only.
- No risk flag threshold is introduced yet.
- No acceptance/rollback behavior uses this metric in v1.0.72.
- Export pipeline is unchanged.

## Validation plan

After CI and merge, rerun the same real brown/black sample and compare the five footprint fields against visually cleaner samples. Only after several real samples establish useful separation should a later version introduce a risk flag, acceptance guard, or targeted reconstruction using this metric.
