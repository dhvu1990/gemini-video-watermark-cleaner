## Evidence from the v1.0.107 seven-scene batch

The newly deployed v1.0.107 batch preview was compared with the prior seven-scene preview. The cleaned thumbnails remained visually and, for the reliably alignable scenes, pixel-near-identical after browser-scale alignment. The visible diamond contour therefore did not materially improve.

Observed status:

- scene1: strong full dark diamond contour remains; diagnostics report a post-chain watermark residual rather than scene protection.
- scene2: lower/right contour remains and is marked scene-protected.
- scene3: strong full contour remains and is marked scene-protected.
- scene4: residual contour remains around genuine curved structure; the real structure is preserved.
- scene5: faint central/lower ghost remains and is marked scene-protected.
- scene6: strong contour remains behind a genuine bright crossing line.
- scene7: lower/central ghost remains and is marked scene-protected.

This confirms that v1.0.107 deployed correctly but its new partial mode remained too difficult to enter. It is not a stale GitHub Pages or preview/export-parity problem.

## Root causes addressed

### 1. Global crossing metrics still vetoed localized safe work

The outline measurement and candidate pass already apply `sceneEdgeProtectionAt()` per pixel. Even so, v1.0.107 required low global crossing score, edge density and continuity density before partial mode could run. A localized genuine line, or an imperfectly registered residual diamond, could therefore block the whole pass.

v1.0.108 treats global crossing metrics only as a dense-complexity veto. Eligibility is driven primarily by strong multi-sector watermark-contour evidence that remains after guarded pixels have been excluded. The candidate itself keeps a stricter per-pixel scene guard, lower strength, lower blend and lower luma delta.

### 2. Strong closed contours could remain blocked by the broad-body gate

scene1 is scene-safe but still retains a strong four-sided contour. The earlier weak-body gate can reject this case when body measurement coverage is dense even though the remaining work is confined to low-alpha contour pixels.

v1.0.108 adds an explicit production-only contour-body override. It requires stronger closed multi-sector outline evidence, bounded body score and outline dominance. The override does not broaden the mask: it still edits only low-alpha contour pixels and uses conservative correction limits.

### 3. Diagnostics could not identify the actual blocker

Batch risk flags now distinguish:

- `outline-partial-scene-protection`
- `outline-contour-body-override`
- `outline-scene-gate-blocked`
- `outline-body-gate-blocked`
- `outline-residual-escalation-rejected`

A partial scene is now considered processable for post-chain reporting when `sceneEligible` is true, even though the strict whole-ROI `sceneSafe` value remains false.

## Safety retained

- No detector confidence threshold is lowered.
- No global Body gain or alpha gain is increased.
- No ROI expansion is introduced.
- Genuine scene-edge pixels retain a stricter hard guard in partial mode.
- Candidate acceptance still requires outline improvement and bounded global luma/chroma/total residuals.
- Dense or broadly guarded scene structure remains blocked.
- The existing preview/export calibration parity path is unchanged.
