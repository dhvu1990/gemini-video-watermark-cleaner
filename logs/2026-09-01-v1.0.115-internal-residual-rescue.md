# v1.0.115 - internal residual ghost/highlight rescue

## Real-video evidence
The first v1.0.114 validation batch shows a clear improvement in outer contour cleanup. Scenes 2, 5, 6 and 7 are materially cleaner. Remaining failures are now mostly internal rather than external:

- scene3 keeps a bright internal watermark highlight/spot
- scene1, scene4 and scene8 retain a faint diamond-shaped tone/texture imprint through the body

## Root cause
The v1.0.114 contour safety band intentionally limits its stronger work to low-alpha/external contour pixels. That protects already-good body reconstruction, but it also means a mid/high-alpha watermark imprint can survive. Existing body rescue can additionally be blocked by scene-edge protection when an internal watermark highlight itself creates a local edge.

## Changes
- add a final `internalResidualRescue` stage after contour micro-interpolation
- predict hidden background from multiple opposing clean directions outside the full contour safety band
- measure residuals only inside the canonical mid/high-alpha watermark body
- add a high-residual internal-highlight path for isolated bright/dark leftovers
- allow a bounded scene-edge override only for high residuals with strong multi-direction agreement
- keep normal body ghost cleanup more conservative than the highlight path
- require global total/luma/chroma rollback safety before accepting a candidate
- expose accepted mode, corrected pixels, highlight pixels, score/peak improvement and rejection flags in batch preview

## Safety invariants
- no detector threshold reduction
- no global Body gain increase
- no full-frame inpaint
- no additional outer mask expansion beyond v1.0.114
- normal scene-edge protection remains active
- strong-edge override is limited to high internal residual with low prediction spread and bounded maximum blend/luma delta
- global total/luma/chroma safety remains mandatory

## Validation targets
Primary: scene3, scene1, scene4, scene8.
Regression: scene2, scene5, scene6, scene7.
