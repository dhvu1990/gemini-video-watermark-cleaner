# v1.0.116 - Post-internal contour dissolve

## Trigger
User validation screenshot shows the Gemini diamond outline remains visibly traced in all eight cleaned previews. v1.0.115 reduced internal ghost/highlight residue but did not fully dissolve the geometric contour.

## Change
- Add a second contour micro-interpolation stage after internal residual rescue.
- Lower the residual trigger for thin post-chain outlines.
- Expand the handled alpha range from the first-pass 0.28 ceiling to 0.42 for the final contour stage.
- Allow up to two bounded contour passes with max luma delta 9 and max blend 0.36.
- Keep scene-edge protection and the existing local/global rollback checks.
- Preserve the original first contour pass and internal rescue ordering.

## Goal
Remove the visible diamond border by interpolating across the watermark contour into surrounding image content without broad blur and without overwriting protected scene/text edges.
