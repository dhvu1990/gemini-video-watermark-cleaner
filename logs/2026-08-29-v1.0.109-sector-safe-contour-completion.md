# v1.0.109 — sector-safe contour completion

## Real-video evidence
The v1.0.108 seven-scene batch still leaves recognizable Gemini/Veo contour sectors. The new diagnostics identify the dominant blockers instead of only reporting the final residual:

- scene2/3/4/5/6/7 repeatedly report `outline-scene-gate-blocked`
- scene1 reports `outline-body-gate-blocked` while the remaining artifact is predominantly a thin contour
- genuine crossing lines in scene4/scene6 are still visibly preserved

## Diagnosis
v1.0.108 added the right partial/body rescue paths, but production defaults were still conservative enough that the new paths rarely opened on the real batch.

The remaining issue is not detector location, global body gain, alpha gain, ROI size, or preview/export parity. It is gate calibration after a strong residual contour has already been confirmed.

## Change
Production outline-rescue defaults are tuned without increasing the normal cleanup strength:

- scene-guarded contour allowance: 0.34 -> 0.52
- partial guarded contour allowance: 0.34 -> 0.52
- partial minimum outline score: 1.22 -> 1.18
- partial safe contour ratio: 0.58 -> 0.48
- partial safe contour pixels: 18 -> 16
- partial safe sample density: 0.045 -> 0.035
- contour-body override minimum outline score: 1.35 -> 1.25
- contour-body override minimum sectors: 4 -> 3
- contour-body override max body score: 6.0 -> 8.0
- contour-body override minimum dominance: 0.42 -> 0.30

Normal outline strength remains 0.58. Partial/body override modes retain their lower strength/blend/luma caps and per-pixel scene-edge guard.

## Safety constraints retained
- no detector threshold reduction
- no global Body gain increase
- no alpha gain increase
- no ROI expansion
- no full-footprint repaint
- per-pixel scene-edge protection remains active
- candidate rollback still requires outline improvement and non-regression of total/luma/chroma residuals

## Validation target
After CI passes and v1.0.109 is deployed, rerun the same seven-scene batch. Expected outcome:

1. scene1 should stop reporting `outline-body-gate-blocked` and should at least attempt the conservative contour-only override.
2. scene3/4/5/6/7 should more often enter `outline-partial-scene-protection` instead of being globally scene-blocked.
3. scene4/scene6 genuine crossing lines must remain visually intact.
4. If an attempted candidate is rejected, diagnostics should show `outline-residual-escalation-rejected`, which is the next tuning signal rather than another global gate block.
