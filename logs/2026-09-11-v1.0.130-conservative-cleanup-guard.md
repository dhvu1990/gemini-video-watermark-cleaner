# v1.0.130 - Conservative cleanup guard after fresh 8-video validation

Fresh 8-video batch validation on 2026-09-11 showed that watermark localization remained reliable, but visual cleanup quality was inversely correlated with detector confidence in several cases. In particular, scene3/scene4/scene5 at roughly 91-92% detection confidence developed dark/brown or smeared watermark-shaped blotches, while scene2/scene7 around 47-51% confidence were visibly more natural.

## Root cause direction

Detection confidence had leaked into restoration aggressiveness in the late cleanup chain. High-confidence inputs could receive stronger contour/body/background correction even though detector confidence only establishes how sure the detector is about watermark identity/location; it is not evidence that the hidden scene can safely tolerate a stronger reconstruction.

The current v1.0.129 temporal atlas is retained. It already supports motion-aligned clean-exposure donors and is the preferred source of true scene pixels when motion exposes the watermark footprint.

## v1.0.130 changes

- Added physical artifact measurements to `smoothRebuildArtifactGuard`:
  - mean absolute luma movement;
  - signed luma movement;
  - dark-shift and severe-dark-shift footprint fractions;
  - before/after local edge energy and edge-energy ratio.
- The guard can now reject destructive tone shifts and scene-structure collapse independently of watermark residual-score metrics.
- Late persistent-contour cleanup now separates observed detector confidence from cleanup confidence.
- All normal late contour/background reconstruction uses a fixed conservative cleanup confidence (`0.55`) instead of becoming stronger at 0.8-0.9+ detector confidence.
- Actual detector confidence remains available for detector gating and diagnostics.
- The v1.0.127 broad high-confidence body residual pass is now opt-in only in normal runtime because fresh real-world validation showed it can create large dark/blended body artifacts on textured scenes.
- Added a whole late-chain quality rollback. If the final late candidate introduces destructive darkening or collapses scene structure, the chain returns its untouched input instead of accepting the candidate.
- Added natural numeric batch filename comparison helpers so scene numbers can be ordered as scene1, scene2, ... scene10 rather than lexical order.

## Safety priority

For this release the cleanup priority is:

1. preserve scene structure;
2. prevent dark/brown blotches and destructive tone shifts;
3. remove the remaining watermark residual.

A faint residual is preferred over a visibly damaged background.

## Regression coverage

Added tests requiring:

- broad 18-level darkening across the watermark footprint to roll back even when residual-score checks are disabled;
- small conservative tone movement to remain accepted;
- the late wrapper to use fixed cleanup confidence and opt-in body rescue;
- numeric batch scene ordering helpers to produce human scene order.

## Validation policy

Synthetic tests and CI verify invariants only. Final acceptance still requires a new real 8-video batch from the user; previous visual results are evidence for the fix but are not reused as a pass result.
