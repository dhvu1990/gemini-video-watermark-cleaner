# v1.0.68 Real Preview Observations

Date: 2026-08-18

## Scope

Visual review of seven user-supplied auto-detection preview screenshots produced by v1.0.68. This is a documentation-only observation pass; no source, threshold, mask, donor selection, calibration logic, or pixel behavior is changed.

## Evidence

The supplied screenshots compare `ZOOMED ORIGINAL` and `ZOOMED CLEANED` regions for several portrait Gemini Veo samples. No exported `antiStreak` telemetry values were supplied with these screenshots, so the findings below are visual observations only and must not be treated as telemetry-confirmed attribution.

## Visual findings by sample

1. Dark structured background with a curved gold line crossing the watermark: watermark body is removed well; the underlying line is largely preserved. A faint shape-aligned/curved residual remains around the former watermark footprint.
2. Dark structured background with a narrow gold line: cleanup is strong and the line remains readable. Minor local blur/halo remains near the crossing.
3. Near-black low-detail background: result is very clean; visible watermark body and outline are effectively removed in the preview.
4. Dark structured background with several diagonal gold lines: the lines survive the cleanup, but local texture/line continuity is imperfect and there are small smear/brightness inconsistencies around the former footprint.
5. Dark background with multiple curved bright lines: watermark body is removed and the main curves are preserved; residual blur/low-frequency mismatch remains around the lower/central footprint.
6. Brown/black structured material boundary: clear low-frequency geometric/shading footprint remains after cleanup. This is one of the strongest remaining failures: the watermark body is gone, but the cleaned region still reveals a diamond/triangular shape through tone and texture mismatch.
7. Warm wood/structured surface: watermark body is removed, but the cleaned region still shows a subtle shape-aligned tone/texture mismatch and directional reconstruction trace.

## Cross-sample conclusion

v1.0.68 appears substantially stronger on:
- near-black / smooth backgrounds;
- narrow bright lines crossing the watermark;
- preserving major structured edges without the severe paint-like streaking seen in earlier samples.

The dominant remaining visual problem is no longer generic watermark-body removal. It is a structured-background residual consisting of:
- low-frequency shape-aligned tone mismatch;
- residual geometric footprint / diamond outline;
- local texture orientation or continuity mismatch;
- mild blur/smear around bright structured lines in a minority of samples.

The screenshots do not show a strong recurrence of the earlier severe temporal-donor paint/streak failure. Based on the visual evidence alone, the next investigation should prioritize the structured cleanup path rather than increasing temporal-donor strength or adding another temporal repair filter.

## Recommended v1.0.69 decision gate

Do not change source yet. First inspect real v1.0.68 `antiStreak` telemetry for at least the strongest structured failures (especially samples 6 and 7):
- `riskFlags`
- temporal donor attempted / accepted / rejected
- temporal donor guarded ratio and structure mismatch
- atlas donor count / mean confidence / mean donor spread
- structured aligned before/after score and sample density
- structured stage acceptance states (consensus, shape ghost, center seam, local tone match, outer halo)

If temporal flags remain low while structured residual flags are high, v1.0.69 should target a structured low-frequency shape/tone residual diagnostic or guard rather than another temporal filter.

## Risk

Visual screenshots alone cannot prove which runtime stage caused the remaining artifacts. Any algorithm change before reading telemetry would risk solving the wrong layer.
