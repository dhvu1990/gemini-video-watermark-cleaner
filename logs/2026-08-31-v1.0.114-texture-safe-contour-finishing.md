# v1.0.114 — texture-safe contour finishing

## Evidence
Recent v1.0.113 batches show strong results on smooth/empty backgrounds and useful micro-interpolation acceptance, but natural foliage, gravel, rough stone and other dense textures still leave a visible thin Gemini/Veo contour. Global gain increases would risk smearing real texture.

## Goal
Allow the existing narrow contour micro-interpolation pass to keep a useful repair on texture-heavy scenes only when local texture energy is preserved.

## Changes
- measure local luma-edge texture energy before/after on corrected contour pixels
- classify a corrected band as rough texture only above a minimum local energy
- add `texture-safe` acceptance after normal outline-wide and local-band acceptance
- require local residual improvement, low mean blend, bounded scene-guarded fraction and preserved texture-energy ratio
- permit only a narrow luma rollback relaxation for this mode; total/chroma limits stay unchanged
- allow at most a 0.2% outline-score tolerance for texture-safe finishing

## Safety invariants
- no detector threshold change
- no Body gain or alpha gain increase
- no ROI expansion
- no full-area inpaint
- hard scene guard remains 0.40
- strength remains 0.42
- max blend remains 0.30
- max luma delta remains 7
- texture-safe acceptance still requires local improvement and global rollback checks

## Validation target
Re-run the foliage/gravel/rough-surface batch plus previously clean empty/text cases. Desired result: more accepted finishing on texture-heavy contour residuals with no visible texture flattening or text/real-edge damage.
