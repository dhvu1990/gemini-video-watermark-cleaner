# v1.0.110 — Guarded contour rescue

## Evidence from the new v1.0.109 batch

The new seven-scene batch confirms that v1.0.109 opened some intended paths but still leaves visible diamond sectors on structured scenes.

Observed diagnostics:
- scene1 reaches `outline-partial-scene-protection` but is still stopped by `outline-body-gate-blocked`.
- scene2/scene3/scene7 reach `outline-contour-body-override` but are still stopped by `outline-scene-gate-blocked`.
- scene4/scene5 remain blocked by both scene/body gates.
- scene6 is a clean success with `Safe empty-zone hard suppression`; the cleaned preview is effectively black and should not be made more aggressive.

## Interpretation

The remaining bottleneck is no longer detector confidence or preview/export parity. It is the conservative contour finishing gate: too much of the contour is classified as scene-guarded, and several strong residual contours still fail the body override.

## Change

Production contour completion now:
- allows up to 68% scene-guarded contour coverage for the finishing gate;
- lowers the minimum unguarded contour ratio to 30%;
- tolerates moderately higher global scene-edge density only in the partial protected path;
- simultaneously lowers partial correction strength/blend/luma caps and hard scene-guard threshold;
- allows higher measured body score / lower outline dominance only for the contour-only override, while lowering its per-pixel correction caps;
- exposes precise partial/body sub-gate blockers in batch diagnostics.

## Non-goals / safety

No detector threshold reduction, no Body gain increase, no alpha gain increase, no ROI expansion, and no stronger normal outline pass. Existing per-pixel scene-edge protection and before/after rollback acceptance remain mandatory.

## Real-video validation target

Re-run the same seven-scene batch after deployment. The primary success criterion is visible reduction of remaining watermark sectors in scene1–5/7 without changing genuine scene lines. scene6 must remain a clean pass.
