# v1.0.117 - Texture-safe contour dissolve

## Trigger
User validation of v1.0.116 shows two remaining failure modes: visible Gemini diamond contour on multiple scenes, and destructive texture artifacts/black or colored streaks on structured backgrounds.

## Change
- Replace the final symmetric contour interpolation pass with outer-only directional regression.
- Use only clean samples outside the watermark safety band, never the inner watermark body, for the final contour prediction.
- Probe several outward ray angles and prefer the locally most coherent texture direction.
- Reduce strength/blend automatically on structured textures while allowing stronger correction on smooth fields.
- Add local artifact veto using clean-sample luma range/median checks before committing each corrected pixel.
- Keep scene-edge protection and global rollback checks including dark-candidate and clipping guards.
- Preserve the earlier contour/interior rescue pipeline and run this pass only at the final contour stage.

## Goal
Dissolve the remaining diamond border into the surrounding scene without the black holes, orange streaks, or texture tearing seen in structured-background validation frames.
