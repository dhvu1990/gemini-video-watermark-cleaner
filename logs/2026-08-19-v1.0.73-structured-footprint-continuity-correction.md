## Root cause

The v1.0.72 continuity probe followed the alpha/watermark normal direction. That direction is appropriate for crossing the watermark boundary, but it is not the direction in which a genuine scene edge continues.

A real material edge continues along the tangent of the image edge. For a vertical scene boundary, the image gradient points horizontally while the edge itself continues vertically. The old probe moved horizontally into flat regions and therefore under-reported continuity.

## Correction

`src/video/structuredFootprintDiagnostics.js` now:
- derives the local image-edge tangent from the low-pass image gradient;
- probes in both tangent directions;
- still requires the probe to reach pixels outside the watermark alpha footprint;
- compares outside-gradient orientation and magnitude with the local image gradient;
- keeps the existing continuity discount strength and synthetic assertion thresholds.

The correction does **not** weaken the failed assertion. The scene-boundary fixture must now produce the intended continuity evidence.
