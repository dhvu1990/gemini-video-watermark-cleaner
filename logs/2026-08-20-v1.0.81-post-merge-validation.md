## Runtime change summary

v1.0.81 changes the final evidence-gated structured refinement candidate only.

Instead of repeating another broad structured ring pass, the final refinement now:

1. requires residual geometry aligned with the watermark alpha gradient;
2. requires the residual sign to remain coherent at the center and both neighboring tangent samples;
3. uses anchor and tangent disagreement as real-scene structure guards;
4. applies luma-only bounded micro-correction;
5. optionally applies a conservative local-tone micro pass;
6. measures the resulting candidate;
7. accepts it only if aligned residual improves and total/luma/chroma residuals remain inside strict safety limits.

No global structured cleanup strength was increased.

## Important interpretation

A risk flag remaining after v1.0.81 is not automatically a failure. The primary criterion is visual improvement without damaging real scene structure.

Likewise, the refinement can correctly reject its candidate. A rejected candidate means the safety gates preferred the prior pixels rather than forcing an unsafe cleanup.
