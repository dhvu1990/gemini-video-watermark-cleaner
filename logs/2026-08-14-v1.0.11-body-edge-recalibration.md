# v1.0.11 - Body/Edge Recalibration Regression Fix

Date: 2026-08-14

## Trigger

Real-browser testing of v1.0.10 on a 1080x1920 Gemini/Veo sample showed:

- detector confidence remained high (~88% match)
- detected position remained correct at the known 72px portrait inset location
- `ZOOMED ORIGINAL` clearly showed the Gemini diamond
- `ZOOMED CLEANED` over-darkened the center of the watermark while the outline remained visible
- calibration selected approximately:
  - profile: `96`
  - shape scale: `1.000`
  - edge gain: `1.00`
  - edge boost: `0.030`
  - body gain: about `1.125`
  - residual score: about `16.137`

This demonstrated that the v1.0.10 objective could reward a low residual-gradient score even when the center was visibly over-corrected.

## Root cause

v1.0.10 fixed Body gain to the detector's initial alpha-gain estimate and only searched profile/shape/edge parameters. The scoring objective emphasized residual edge energy and outside-footprint damage, but it did not strongly penalize a cleaned center that became much darker than the surrounding background.

A second risk existed during export: adaptive per-frame gain mixed the calibrated/requested gain with a fresh local estimate and could drift back toward an overly aggressive value.

## v1.0.11 changes

### Independent Body gain search

Before profile/shape/edge search, calibration now evaluates a bounded Body gain set derived from the initial estimate:

- 0.78x estimate
- 0.88x estimate
- 0.98x estimate
- 1.08x estimate
- explicit neutral candidate 1.0

All candidates are clamped to 0.55-1.35.

### Background continuity objective

A new `backgroundContinuityScore()` predicts the underlying background inside the watermark from low-alpha anchor pixels found horizontally and vertically outside the watermark footprint.

Candidate cleanup is penalized when:

- cleaned pixels differ strongly from the interpolated nearby background
- pixels clip close to black/white while the predicted background is not clipped
- cleanup changes pixels outside the alpha footprint

The final candidate objective uses background continuity as the primary term and residual-edge energy as a secondary term.

### Bounded edge search retained

After Body gain selection, the existing 54 shape candidates are still tested:

- 2 alpha profiles
- 3 shape scales
- 3 edge boosts
- 3 edge gains

This keeps runtime bounded while allowing the edge footprint to be optimized separately from the center strength.

### Export gain stability

Adaptive Body gain now:

- weights the calibrated/requested gain more heavily than the local per-frame estimate
- remains within +/-0.06 of the calibrated gain
- changes by no more than +/-0.025 per frame

This prevents a visually acceptable preview from drifting toward a darker result during full-video export.

### Diagnostics

Detection now reports both:

- `estimatedAlphaGain`: the original detector estimate
- `alphaGain`: the final calibrated Body gain

Calibration metadata also records initial Body gain, baseline score and improvement for debugging.

## Regression tests

Added tests to verify:

- body-gain candidates include values below an overestimated detector gain
- background-continuity scoring penalizes an over-clean/dark center more than a correctly restored center
- existing shape-scale, edge-gain and residual-edge tests remain in place
