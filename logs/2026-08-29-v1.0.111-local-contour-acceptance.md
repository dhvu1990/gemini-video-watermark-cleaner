# v1.0.111 — local contour acceptance

## Real-video finding
The v1.0.110 batch shows that the intended conservative finishing paths now activate on several difficult scenes, but many candidates are rejected at the final outline acceptance stage. Visually, scene1/2/3/5/7 improve, scene4 remains difficult, and scene6 should remain protected.

## Change
- Keep normal outline strength/gain unchanged.
- Relax only conservative acceptance thresholds:
  - partial protected min improvement: 0.012
  - partial protected max outline ratio: 0.988
  - contour-body override min improvement: 0.015
  - contour-body override max outline ratio: 0.985
- Preserve conservative strength/blend caps from v1.0.110.
- Add rejection diagnostics for too few corrected pixels, mean blend, local improvement, outline ratio, global total, global luma, and global chroma.

## Safety
No detector threshold reduction, no Body gain or alpha gain increase, no ROI expansion, no stronger normal outline pass, and no relaxation of global total/luma/chroma rollback limits.

## Validation target
Rerun the same representative real-video batch after deployment. Expect fewer `outline-residual-escalation-rejected` cases when local contour improvement is real and globally safe. If a rejection remains, the new flags should identify the exact acceptance condition that stopped it.
