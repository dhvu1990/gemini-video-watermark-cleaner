# v1.0.132 — Veo text and shadow reconstruction

## Evidence and root cause

The operator's screenshot from v1.0.131 showed correct Veo text localization (~53% match) but the ZOOMED CLEANED preview had a high-contrast orange/dark outline. The existing text-only inverse-alpha step used a rough hand-made white-letter stencil. It neither matched the real strokes accurately nor covered the dark/shadow pixels around them. Increasing alpha globally would worsen dark undershoot.

## Change

- Preserve the existing Veo and Gemini detector geometry/thresholds.
- Keep the Gemini diamond reconstruction chain entirely unchanged.
- Separate the Veo detection stencil from a dedicated `veoWordmarkRepair.js` branch.
- Build a bounded local wordmark stroke mask from pixel-wise positive luminance evidence relative to a robust, second-order exterior donor model; expand slightly to include the dark outline.
- Require three-part letter evidence, sufficient clean exterior donors and bounded exterior texture complexity.
- Reconstruct through a *gradient-domain, texture-preserving* donor from clean pixels above the mark, with the original nearby scene pixels as fixed boundaries.
- Protect crossing real scene lines through two-sided exterior gradient evidence. Keep the original ROI when evidence is absent or a structure/quality guard rejects the repair.
- Expose `veoWordmark.accepted`, corrected pixels, background fit, support and a rejection reason in the inspection preview; replace misleading Gemini-specific “Structured-background fallback” text for Veo.
- Keep the exact specialized repair function shared by preview and MP4 export. No SynthID or hidden provenance changes.

## Real authorized video validation

The operator's privately supplied 1920×1080, 8-second, 24 fps Veo sample was analyzed **locally only** (never committed). Selected 67×67 ROIs from different times, including the first, middle and final seconds, were visually compared as original vs prototype-restored. Original full-frame screenshots and source video remain outside the public repository.

A local Node.js run of the new reconstruction function against all 192 source-frame ROIs accepted 192/192. The right-side text and dark edge were much less visible than in v1.0.131, with real brown surface texture better preserved than a flat polynomial interpolation. Test reference `Mean positive high-pass` in the wordmark region fell from roughly 10–13 to 1–3 on seven inspected frames.

**Limitations:** this source-video ROI comparison is not yet a browser-encoded MP4 export. Some later frames have a nearby moving bright reflection/line; inspect temporal flicker and scene texture after export. Acceptance and the numeric reduction are not guarantees of no remaining artifact. The method intentionally withholds cleanup on complex exterior structure or crossing scene edges.
